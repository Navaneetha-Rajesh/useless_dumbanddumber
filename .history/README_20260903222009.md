## Project Description 
 
**Ayalathe Ammavan** is an AI-powered virtual uncle who sits on your screen and does what every neighbourhood uncle does best — **watch, judge, and give completely unsolicited advice.** 
 
Using your webcam and computer vision, Ammavan keeps an eye on your posture and phone usage and reacts accordingly. Because productivity is apparently not possible without an aunty's neighbour's brother's cousin monitoring you. 
 
--- 
 
## The Problem (that doesn't exist) 
 
Modern problems include: 
 
- Sitting incorrectly for 5 hours straight. 
- Looking at your phone when you are supposed to be working. 
- Accidentally taking a "small break" that lasts 47 minutes. 
- Falling asleep in front of your laptop. 
- Nobody around to say **"നിവർന്ന് ഇരിക്ക് മോനേ!"** 
 
Clearly, society has failed us. 
 
We identified this critical gap in the market: 
 
> **What if a random Malayalam uncle could monitor your life choices in real time?** 
 
Nobody asked for it. 
 
We built it anyway.

# Implementation 
 
## Software 
 
The application runs completely in the browser. 
 
The webcam feed is processed using TensorFlow.js. MoveNet is used to estimate the user's body pose, while COCO-SSD identifies objects such as mobile phones. 
 
Based on the detected state, Ammavan changes his expression, displays Malayalam dialogue, and reacts accordingly.
